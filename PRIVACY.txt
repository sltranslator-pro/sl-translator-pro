SL TRANSLATOR PRO - PRIVACY NOTICE AND TERMS
Last updated: October 22, 2026

SL Translator Pro is free and open-source client software. The hosted translation service is operated separately.

DATA SENT TO THE SERVICE
- Chat text that needs translation
- Requested language information
- License key and client version
- Temporary session identifier and translation status
- Device identifier derived from Windows MachineGuid for access control

DATA THAT MAY BE STORED
- License status and device binding
- Avatar UUID/name when provided during activation or support
- Translation logs including source and translated text, language pair, timing and result status
- Network and security metadata

Under the current retention policy, message text in new operational records is scheduled for removal after up to 7 days. Diagnostic metadata may be kept longer. Some older records created before the current policy may have been retained longer for quality research.

The official service uses self-hosted translation models. It does not send chat text to Google Translate, Microsoft Translator, OpenAI or DeepL. Cloudflare may process network traffic, and the client contacts GitHub for update checks.

TERMS
- The software and hosted service are provided free of charge and "as is", without warranty.
- Translation accuracy and uninterrupted availability are not guaranteed.
- Do not use the service for spam, abuse or illegal activity.
- Access keys may be disabled to protect the service from abuse or excessive automated traffic.
- Modified client builds are not official releases and may behave differently.
