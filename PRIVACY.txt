SL TRANSLATOR PRO - PRIVACY NOTICE AND TERMS
Last updated: October 23, 2026

SL Translator Pro is free and open-source client software for Firestorm.

ON YOUR COMPUTER
The client stores nothing during normal use: no chat history and no message log.
If you turn on the optional /tr debug command, a diagnostic log is written to
%TEMP%\slt_debug.log and can include translated messages. Turn debug off to stop it.

SENT FOR TRANSLATION (to api.sltranslate.com over HTTPS)
- The message text and the target language
- Your license key, client version and a temporary session identifier
- Whether translation is on or off
- A device identifier derived from Windows MachineGuid, used to bind your free key to one computer

The translation service does not forward your messages to Google, Microsoft, OpenAI or DeepL.
The client also contacts GitHub to check for updates.
